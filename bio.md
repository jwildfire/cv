# Bio

Jeremy Wildfire is a Data Scientist at Gilead, where he leads technical strategy for AI-enabled clinical trial analytics. He is the technical lead for Gilead’s enterprise Risk-Based Quality Management initiative and a creator of the open-source RBQM framework good statistical monitoring ({gsm}) and the OpenRBQM PHUSE project. He previously served as founding technical lead of the Interactive Safety Graphics sub-team in the ASA Biopharm–DIA Safety Working Group. His current work focuses on GxP-compliant clinical pipelines that integrate Generative AI.
