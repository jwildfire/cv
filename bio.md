# Bio

Jeremy Wildfire is a Data Scientist at Gilead where his team develops modern open-source pipelines for clinical trial analytics. His current work focuses on building GxP-compliant frameworks utilizing generative AI. He is the technical lead for Gilead’s enterprise Risk-Based Quality Management initiative and a creator of the open-source RBQM framework good statistical monitoring ({gsm}) and the OpenRBQM PHUSE project. He previously served as founding technical lead of the Interactive Safety Graphics team in the ASA Biopharm–DIA Safety Working Group.
